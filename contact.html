What helps us answer quickly

You do not need to send any of this to reach us, but if you include it, we can usually tell you on the first reply whether coverage is likely and roughly what it would cost.

  • What you make or sell, and roughly how much of it is made in the United States.
  • Your approximate total annual sales, and the share that goes to foreign buyers.
  • How much of your foreign business is sold on credit terms, as opposed to prepaid or by letter of credit.
  • Your top five destination countries.
  • The payment terms you typically extend, counted from shipment.

Nothing you send is shared outside the firm. We will not add you to a mailing list.
