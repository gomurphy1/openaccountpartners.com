A brokerage built for the policies nobody else wants to write.

Open Account Partners is a new firm with one market, one product line and a specific idea about how to serve both.

The idea

Export credit insurance for small businesses is a solved product. The coverage exists, the terms are fair, and the government agency that provides it actively wants more small exporters to use it. What is missing is distribution. A broker earns a commission that is a percentage of premium, and a small exporter's premium is small. The work of finding that exporter, qualifying them, preparing the application and reporting shipments every month costs roughly the same whether the policy is for $500,000 of sales or $50 million. So the large brokerages concentrate on large accounts, and tens of thousands of small exporters never hear from anyone.

We think that cost structure can be changed. Most of the work in placing and servicing a small policy is finding information, organizing it and filing it on time. That is work that software now does well. If the finding, qualifying and paperwork are handled by systems rather than by people, a small policy stops being a loss and starts being a client worth keeping for years.

Open Account Partners is being built on that premise: an experienced insurance professional in front of the client, and a purpose-built back office behind him.

How we will work

One market

US small and mid-size exporters selling on open account. We will not take larger accounts, and we will not broker other lines of insurance.

Plain answers

If you are not a fit, we will tell you on the first call. If a different insurer suits you better, we will point you there.

Servicing first

The policy is the easy part. Keeping it current every month for years is the job, and it is the part of the firm we have invested the most in.

Independent

We are a broker. We will represent the exporter, not the insurer, and be paid the same way every insurance broker is paid.
